# AttendanceV2

## Employee Attendance Management System

AttendanceV2 is a web-based employee attendance management system designed to manage employee attendance, movements, permits, delays, and attendance reports.

The project was designed to support structured attendance processing and business rules for employee attendance management.

---

## Project Overview

AttendanceV2 provides an integrated solution for managing employee attendance and related administrative processes.

The system includes attendance processing, employee movements, permit management, delay calculation, attendance status tracking, and reporting.

---

## Key Features

- Employee and department management
- Employee attendance management
- Fingerprint attendance processing
- First-in and last-out attendance tracking
- Morning and evening permit management
- Delay and grace-period calculation
- Attendance status management
- Employee movement management
- Holiday management
- Attendance reports
- Permit summary reports
- Delay and penalty reports
- Role-based access and user management
- Audit and system activity tracking

## Main Modules

### Employee Management
Manage employee information, departments, and employee assignments.

### Attendance Management
Process attendance records and determine daily attendance status based on defined business rules.

### Permit Management
Manage morning and evening permits according to organizational attendance policies.

### Movement Management
Handle official employee movements and attendance-related exceptions.

### Reports
Generate reports related to attendance, permits, delays, and penalties.

### User Management
Manage system users, roles, and access permissions.

---

## Attendance Rules

The system applies configurable attendance rules including:

- Official working hours
- Grace periods
- Morning permits
- Evening permits
- Delay calculation
- Attendance status
- Missing sign-in or sign-out handling
- Official movements and exemptions

The business rules are implemented according to the requirements of the organization.
